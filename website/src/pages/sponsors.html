Support

What a gift to the division buys.

The Sullivans Division is run entirely by volunteers. Families cover enrollment, uniforms and training, and for some families that is the reason a cadet cannot join. Support closes that gap.

Where it goes

Real line items from the unit's own cost sheet, not categories.

  • A cadet's annual dues $150 covers one cadet's enrollment year with the division.
  • A sea bag $75 to $150 for the issued kit a cadet needs before a first training.
  • Meals on a drill weekend $20 feeds one cadet for an overnight drill, $5 for a day drill.
  • Travel to training Transportation to training sites is not covered by any program fee.

Talk to the division first.

There is no online donation form yet, on purpose. Write to the unit and the Commanding Officer will confirm how a gift is received and what recognition the division can offer, before you give anything.
