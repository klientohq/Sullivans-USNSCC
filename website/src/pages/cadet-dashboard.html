Cadets, families, and staff

Access to unit resources.

Some unit material is not published openly: drill details, uniform guidance, announcements, and forms. Access is granted to an approved list of email addresses by unit leadership.

How sign-in works

You enter your email address and receive a one-time code. There is no password to create and none is stored. If your address is on the approved list the code lets you in; if it is not, contact the unit and ask to be added.

This gate is being set up. Until it is live, ask the unit directly for anything you need and it will be sent to you.

Request access from the unit

Official cadet records

Enrollment, advancement, and training records are held nationally in the Naval Sea Cadet Corps system of record, not on this website. This site stores no cadet information of any kind. Reach those records through national Sea Cadets, or ask unit leadership.

Never send a password by email, to the unit or to anyone else.
