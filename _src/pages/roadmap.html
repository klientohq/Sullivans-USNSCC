Modernization roadmap

A cleaner site map for a stronger public presence.

Ryan's recommendations become a phased build plan: clear pages, current information, future cadet tools, and a site that can stay fresh without duplicate posting.

Phase 01

Core Public Pages

Home, About, Leadership, Resource Hub, Contact, and My Account preview.

Phase 02

Unit Operations

Departments, JSCTC Buffalo, Cruise Book, Sponsors, and Donations.

Phase 03

Live Content

Facebook and Instagram feed integration so the site updates with the unit's social activity.

Phase 04

Protected Tools

Cadet dashboard, resources, announcements, forms, and later store checkout functionality.
