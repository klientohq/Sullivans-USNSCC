Unit essentials

Handled by the division

Cadet ID cards, drill meals, the sea bag, and uniform items are issued by the division and handed over at drill rather than shipped. Ask the unit for current pricing and to arrange payment.

  • Cadet ID card

    Issued by the division for new and renewing cadets.

  • Drill meals

    Meal plan covering scheduled drill weekends.

  • Sea bag

    The standard issue kit bag cadets carry to training.

  • Uniform items

    Uniform components issued and fitted through the unit.
