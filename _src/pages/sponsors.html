Sponsors & Support

Fuel the next generation of leaders.

The Sullivans Division is a volunteer-run unit of the U.S. Naval Sea Cadet Corps in Buffalo, New York. Every dollar from our sponsors goes directly to cadets: training, travel, uniforms, and the experiences that build discipline, service, and confidence.

Become a Sponsor See Partnership Levels

Why it matters

Your support reaches cadets directly.

I

Training & gear

Uniforms, safety equipment, and hands-on instruction in seamanship, leadership, and first aid.

II

Travel & opportunity

Transportation and fees so cadets can attend regional trainings, ceremonies, and drills they could not reach alone.

III

Community service

Color guards, parades, and memorials that put cadets in front of the Buffalo community all year long.
