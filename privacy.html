Privacy

This is a youth programme, so the shortest honest answer is the one that matters: this website does not collect anything about you or your child. The detail below says how that is true of each part of the site.

Last reviewed 26 August 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no logins, no newsletter list, and no forms that submit personal details to us. We hold no cadet records of any kind on this site.

Cookies

None are set by us, and none are set by anything on the page unless you ask for it. That is why there is no cookie banner here: there is nothing to consent to until you choose it. The only thing stored in your browser by default is a note that you dismissed the announcement strip at the top of the page, which stays on your device, clears when you close the tab, and is never sent anywhere.

The one exception is the map, and it is opt in. A Google map sets a Google cookie the moment it loads, so we do not load one. The Home and Contact pages show a panel with a Show the map button instead, and nothing reaches Google until you press it. If you would rather not, the "See the park on a map" link opens Google Maps in its own tab, where their rules apply and ours stop.

The contact form

The form builds a message and opens it in your own email app. You read it and you press send, so the message travels from your mail account to info@thesullivansusnscc.com exactly like any other email you write. Nothing you type is stored on this website or passed to a third party.

Before the draft opens, the form checks that a person rather than a bot filled it in, using Cloudflare Turnstile. Turnstile is a challenge, not a profile: it sets no cookie and does not track you across other sites. Our server checks the result of that challenge and keeps no record of the check or of your message.

Visitor numbers

We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics so we know whether recruiting is reaching anyone. It is cookie-free and it does not fingerprint your device or follow you to other websites. What we can see is aggregate: how many people opened a page, roughly where in the world they were, and which link sent them. We cannot see who you are.

Children

This site is written for parents and guardians, and it collects nothing from anyone of any age, so there is nothing here for a child to give away. Enrollment happens on the national Sea Cadets website, not on ours, and any information a family enters there is handled under that programme's own policy.

Links and embeds that are not ours

The store shows merchandise images served by our print partner, and several pages link out to national Sea Cadets, to Google Maps, and to our social profiles. Once you follow one of those, you are on someone else's site under their rules, not ours. The map is the only third-party thing that can run inside a page here, and only after you press its button. Buying merchandise happens entirely on the print partner's storefront, and we never see or hold a payment card.

Photographs

The photographs on this site are of cadets of The Sullivans Division, published with the unit's permission. Location and camera data is stripped from every image before it goes up. If you are a parent and you want a photograph of your cadet taken down, write to us and it comes down.

Asking us about this

Questions about anything on this page go to info@thesullivansusnscc.com. If we change how the site works, we change this page at the same time.
